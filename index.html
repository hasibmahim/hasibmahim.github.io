Current MSc thesis · 2026

When the Logged Item Is Not the Consumed Item:
Representation-Aligned Feedback Correction

InstitutionUniversity of Oulu

Core research question

When a review describes a recipe that the user modified before eating it, should that mismatch be corrected as an error in the item representation, or treated as residual uncertainty in the outcome?

The central idea: the correct treatment of a described item change depends on the change relative to the model representation — not on the edit alone.

01

Historical evidence, corrected before updating

Reviews are decomposed into preference stance, recipe edits, edit motive, and semantic reliability. In-representation edits can shift the expected item state; out-of-representation effects are routed to residual uncertainty rather than being blindly mean-corrected.

Bayesian updatingACSAReliabilityMeasurement error
02

Leakage-proof temporal evaluation

The target rating and target review remain sealed. The primary Track A analysis uses one target per user, with user-level and temporal separation, so every prediction is based strictly on historical evidence available before the target time.

One target / userTemporal splitUser separation
03

Selective dislike prediction on low-health recipes

The downstream task is a post-selection intervention: after a user opens a NutriScore D/E recipe, the model estimates dislike risk and either makes a prediction or abstains. Calibration controls a monotone false-discovery-style risk, while selective risk and coverage are reported separately.

Selective predictionCRCRisk–coverageα = 0.05
04

Model comparison designed to isolate the contribution

The study compares a ratings-only baseline, uncorrected review features, automatic correction, reliability-weighted correction, and manual/oracle correction on the annotated subset. Representation interventions test whether an edit changes category when its corresponding feature is removed.

Ratings-onlyAutomaticReliability-weightedOracle
Data integrity

Ratings 2.5 are excluded from rating-based outcomes; their review text can remain as historical evidence. Rating 6 records are also excluded from ordinal outcomes.

Scope boundary

Realised health is outside the main MSc claim. Without a validated mapping from recipe edits to nutrient changes, the study does not claim health-as-consumed.

Interpretation

This is not an open-catalogue recommender guarantee. It is retrospective/selective prediction over observed low-health interactions, framed as a post-selection intervention.
